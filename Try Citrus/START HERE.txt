CITRUS — IMAGE CONVERTER

DRAG TO CONVERT
1. Drag Sample.jpg in Explorer, then tap Shift.
   Holding Shift before dragging also works.
2. Keep holding the mouse. Release Shift when the wheel appears.
3. Move onto PNG, BMP, or TIFF. The selected wedge turns orange.
4. Release the mouse to convert. The result appears in this folder.

Drag one of your PNG results to see JPG, BMP, and TIFF choices.
The center and gaps are neutral. Escape cancels.

FILE PICKER
1. Double-click Citrus.exe.
2. Click Choose images… and select Sample.jpg or your own images.
3. Select Output format, then click Convert.

SUPPORTED: JPG/JPEG, PNG, BMP, and single-page TIF/TIFF.
Mixed batches offer only formats shared by every selected image.
JPG/BMP put transparent areas on white. Originals are preserved.
Repeated conversions create numbered files instead of overwriting.
Sample.jpg is a small solid-color test image, not a photograph.

BACKGROUND AND SETTINGS
Closing with X keeps Citrus active in the tray.
Right-click the orange-and-leaf icon for Settings:
- Shift, Alt or Control trigger.
- JPEG quality, appearance and reduced animation.
- Pause drag-to-convert or toggle Start with Windows.
- Exit Citrus to fully stop the app.
Keep this folder in place so Windows startup can find Citrus.exe.
